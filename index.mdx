## Key features

- **Log search**: full-text search over logs stored in S3-compatible object storage
- **Log frequency histogram**: visualize log volume and log level distribution over time
- **Quick filters**: one-click filtering by field values with aggregation counts
- **Saved views**: bookmark and share frequently used searches with your team
- **Log export**: download filtered results as NDJSON, CSV, or plain text
- **Log ingestion**: push logs over OTLP or the NDJSON gateway
- **Traces**: ingest OpenTelemetry spans over OTLP, link them to the logs they produced, and search them by service, operation, duration, and status
- **Service health**: request rate, error rate, latency, downstream calls, and failing spans per service and operation, derived from your spans
- **Pull-based sources**: ingest from Amazon Kinesis, Apache Kafka, or SQS
- **Index management**: create, configure, and delete custom indexes and their sources
- **Programmatic access**: personal and service-account API keys for read-only log queries from scripts, dashboards, and agents
- **User management**: invite-based access control with optional Google, GitHub, or OpenID Connect sign-in
