---
title: 'Enable Google OAuth'
description: 'Set up Google OAuth credentials and configure allowed email domains so your team can sign in to Rootprint using their Google accounts.'
---

Google sign-in admits users whose email is in one of your allowed domains. [Authentication overview](/configuration/authentication) has the rules shared by every provider.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create OAuth credentials in Google Cloud Console">
    You need a Google Cloud project. Open the [Google Cloud Console credentials page](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials) and create an OAuth client ID:

    - **Application type:** Web application
    - **Authorized redirect URI:** `https://<your-rootprint>/api/auth/callback/google`

    Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter credentials in Rootprint">
    In Rootprint, go to **Settings → Authentication**, then click **Configure** on the **Google** row. Enter:

    - **Client ID** and **Client Secret**: from the Google Cloud Console.
    - **Allowed domains**: the email domains whose users can sign in. For example, `company.com` admits `alice@company.com` but blocks `user@gmail.com`.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the configuration">
    Click **Save**. A **Continue with Google** button appears on the sign-in page.

  </Step>
</Steps>
