---
title: 'Manage users'
description: 'Invite teammates, assign admin or user roles, reset passwords, and remove accounts from the Rootprint admin UI.'
---

Admins manage accounts in **Settings → Users**: invite users, change roles, reset passwords, and delete accounts.

<Note>
	Rootprint never sends email. Invites and password resets produce a **setup link**. Copy it and
	share it with the person over your own channel (chat, email, a ticket).
</Note>

## Roles

Every account is either an **admin** or a **user**.

| Role      | Can do                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admin** | Everything: open **Settings** to manage users, authentication, indexes, service accounts, and API keys, and **Send data** for setup guides. |
| **User**  | Read logs in every index, and read traces and service health. **Settings** and **Send data** are hidden.  |

## Invite a user

1. Sign in as an admin.
2. Open **Settings → Users**.
3. Click **Invite user**.
4. Enter the person's **name** and **email**, and pick a **role** (admin or user).
5. Click **Create**. Rootprint generates a one-time **setup link**.
6. Copy the link and send it to the person.

When they open the link, Rootprint asks them to choose a password and then signs them in. The account stays inactive until they finish this step. With an external provider enabled, the person can sign in through it with the invited email instead. That consumes the invite without setting a password.

<Note>
	If a setup link is lost or expires before it is used, open the user from the list and click
	**Reissue invite** to generate a fresh one. The old link stops working.
</Note>

## Change a role

Open a user from the list and switch their role between **admin** and **user**. The change takes effect on their next request.

<Warning>
	Keep at least one admin account. If you demote or delete the last admin and lose access, you have
	to recover at the database level — see [Reset admin password](/configuration/reset-admin-password).
</Warning>

## Reset a password

When someone is locked out or you want to rotate their credentials, open the user and click **Reset password**. Rootprint clears the current password, signs the user out of all sessions, and generates a fresh **setup link** for them to choose a new one. Share the link the same way as an invite.

**Reset password** works for every account, including users who only ever signed in through Google, GitHub, or OpenID Connect. For them the setup link adds a password alongside the linked provider. The action disappears while [password sign-in is disabled](/configuration/authentication#password-sign-in).

## Delete a user

Open the user and click **Delete**. This permanently removes the account and everything it owns: sessions, linked OAuth accounts, API keys, saved views, share links, and search preferences. There is no undo.

## Single sign-on users

Users who sign in through [Google](/configuration/google-auth), [GitHub](/configuration/github-auth), or [OpenID Connect](/configuration/oidc-auth) get an account with the **user** role on their first sign-in, with no invite needed. See [Authentication overview](/configuration/authentication#provider-rules).
